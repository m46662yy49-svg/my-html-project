2. Backtest settings

Signal on candle close, simulated entry on next candle open (reduces look-ahead bias).
🎯 Sniper · breakout

Close breaks above prior 20-candle high with volume confirmation.

⚡ Momentum · trend

Positive 7-candle return while close is above 20-candle average.

〰 Dip Hunter · recovery

Price recovers above prior close after a deep pullback below its average.

🐋 Whale Tail · volume anomaly

Unusually high volume plus a positive candle; volume alone is not proof of informed buying.

Default round-trip cost is 1.0% (0.30% fee + 0.20% slippage on entry and exit). Actual costs vary by pool, size, route and network conditions. No gas/priority fee estimate is included.

Methodology & limitations

Transparent rules, not machine-learning predictions

• One position at a time per strategy; no leverage or shorting.

• Entry at next candle open; exit at next valid signal or after 10 candles.

• No look-ahead: rolling indicators use candles available before the signal candle.

• Net trade return deducts estimated entry and exit costs.

• Daily candles can hide intraday volatility and stop-outs.

• Results exclude failed transactions, taxes, MEV, pool migration, rug pulls and delisted pools.

• A single pool is not representative of all markets for a token.
